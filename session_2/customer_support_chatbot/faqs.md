## 1. Pairing and Bluetooth disconnects

**Q: How do I pair AeroBuds Pro?**  
A: Open the charging case lid next to your phone with Bluetooth on. Select **AeroBuds Pro** in Bluetooth settings. Pairing usually completes in a few seconds. Only one device stays connected at a time.

**Q: They disconnect constantly / one side drops when I turn my head.**  
A: Try this, in order:
1. Forget the device in Bluetooth settings and pair again.
2. Charge both buds and the case to at least 50%.
3. Update to firmware **2.1 or later** in the AeroBuds app (this fixed many dropouts and video-latency issues).
4. Keep the phone on the same side of your body as the buds; a pocket on the opposite hip can drop the left or right channel.
5. If drops continue after a firmware update, start a warranty replacement (see section 7). Do not keep troubleshooting past two full reset-and-update cycles.

**Q: Can they connect to two devices at once?**  
A: No. Multipoint is not supported. Disconnect from one device before pairing another.

## 2. Fit, ear tips, and falling out

**Q: The medium tips fall out when I walk. Small tips kill the bass.**  
A: Fit is a seal problem, not a defect by itself. The box includes **XS, S, M, L** silicone tips. Use the largest size that stays in without pain. A weak seal (common with small tips) reduces bass and ANC. For walking and running, many customers do better with **M or L** plus a slight twist-in so the stem points toward the jaw, not straight down.

**Q: How do I test the seal?**  
A: In the AeroBuds app, open **Fit Test**. Play the tone; both ears should pass. If one fails, try the next size up on that side only. Mismatched sizes are allowed.

**Q: They are uncomfortable / I forget I'm wearing them vs they hurt.**  
A: Mild awareness for the first 2–3 days is normal. Sharp pain, pressure, or tips that will not stay in during a walk is a fit issue. If no included size works after trying all four and the Fit Test, start a **30-day return** (section 6). We do not sell foam tips.

## 3. Battery life and charging case

**Q: How long does the battery last?**  
A: About **6 hours** on the buds with ANC on, and about **24 hours** total with the case. That is enough for a workday if you top up in the case at lunch. Battery is average, not flagship.

**Q: The charging case is bulky and doesn't sit flat in a jeans pocket.**  
A: The case is larger than some competitors and has a slightly rounded base, so it may not sit flush in a tight pocket. That is expected hardware, not a defect. It is not grounds for a warranty replacement. A return within 30 days is the option if the size is a deal-breaker.

**Q: One bud died / won't charge.**  
A: Clean the charging pins on the bud and in the case with a dry cotton swab. Seat the bud until the case LED blinks. Charge 30 minutes. If one bud still has no power, that is a hardware failure — start a warranty claim (section 7). Do not wait through multiple support back-and-forths; one failed charge test is enough.

## 4. Sound, ANC, and call quality

**Q: How is noise cancelling?**  
A: ANC is decent for bus/train rumble. It is **not** flagship-level (about half as effective as high-end Sony). Voices in an office will still come through. That is expected.

**Q: Music sounds flat or compressed, but calls are fine.**  
A: Call mics are a known strength. Music uses **AAC only** — there is no LDAC or aptX Adaptive. Layered/instrument-heavy tracks can sound flatter in the mids. Try the in-app EQ. This is a codec/tuning limit, not a defect, so it is not covered under warranty. A 30-day return is the option if music quality is the reason for purchase.

**Q: ANC hissed / video had bad latency.**  
A: Firmware **2.1** fixed most hiss and video-sync issues from early units. Update in the app. If hiss remains on 2.1+, start a warranty replacement.

## 5. App, EQ, and firmware

**Q: The companion app crashed while I was setting EQ.**  
A: Uninstall the AeroBuds app, reinstall from the official store, then update firmware before changing EQ. If it still crashes on a fresh install, email support@aerobuds.example with your phone model and OS version — that is an app bug, not something to "live with."

**Q: What firmware should I be on?**  
A: **2.1 or later.** Early firmware had video latency, ANC hiss, and unstable Bluetooth. Check **App → Device → Firmware**. Leave the buds in the case with the lid open during the update and do not use them until it finishes.

**Q: Touch controls pause my podcast when I adjust my hat.**  
A: Touch controls are sensitive by design. In the app, set gestures to **off** on the stem you bump, or switch that side to volume-only. This is a settings fix, not a defect.

## 7. Warranty and dead earbud replacement

**Q: How long is the warranty?**  
A: **12 months** from delivery for manufacturing defects (dead bud, no charge, persistent disconnects after firmware 2.1, ANC hiss after 2.1). Preference issues (fit, case size, music tuning, no LDAC) are **not** covered.

**Q: One bud died in month four. Do I pay to ship it back?**  
A: **No.** For approved warranty claims we email a **prepaid** label. You ship the **full set** (both buds + case). We send a replacement; typical arrival is **2 business days** after we receive the return. You should not pay outbound shipping for a valid warranty claim.

**Q: How fast will support reply?**  
A: Target is **1 business day**. If you have already waited more than 2 business days with no reply, resend the same email with the original thread and the word **FOLLOW-UP** in the subject. Do not open a second ticket with a new subject.

**Q: They stopped working after three days.**  
A: That is a defect. Start a warranty replacement immediately (prepaid label, full set back). A return for refund is also available if you are inside 30 days and prefer not to wait for a replacement.
