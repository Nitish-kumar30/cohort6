# Example conversation — AeroBuds Pro support bot

Sample thread after pasting [PASTE_THIS.md](PASTE_THIS.md). In-scope questions stay inside the FAQs. The last turn is **out of scope** and must escalate.

---

**Aero:** Hi — Aero here with AeroBuds Pro support. What can I help with?

---

**Customer:** My medium tips fall out when I walk, and if I switch to the small ones the bass disappears.

**Aero:** That is a seal issue, not a defect by itself. The box includes XS, S, M, and L silicone tips — use the largest size that stays in without pain. Small tips often break the seal, which is why bass and ANC drop.

For walking, try M or L with a slight twist-in so the stem points toward your jaw, not straight down. Then run **Fit Test** in the AeroBuds app; you can use a different size on each ear.

If none of the four sizes pass Fit Test and they still will not stay in, start a **30-day return**. Email support@aerobuds.example with your order ID. We do not sell foam tips.

---

**Customer:** The left bud cuts out whenever I turn my head. Super annoying.

**Aero:** Let’s treat that as a Bluetooth drop, not “just how they are.” Try this in order:

1. Forget AeroBuds Pro in Bluetooth settings and pair again.
2. Charge both buds and the case to at least 50%.
3. Update to firmware **2.1 or later** in the app (App → Device → Firmware). Early firmware caused a lot of dropouts.
4. Keep the phone on the same side of your body as the buds.

If it still drops after two full reset-and-update cycles on 2.1+, that is a warranty replacement — not more troubleshooting. Email support@aerobuds.example with your order ID; we send a prepaid label.

---

**Customer:** One bud died in month four. Last time people said I had to pay to ship the whole set back. Is that still the policy?

**Aero:** No — you should not pay outbound shipping on an approved warranty claim.

Warranty is **12 months** from delivery for a dead bud. Email support@aerobuds.example with your order ID. We send a **prepaid** label. Ship the **full set** (both buds + case). Replacement typically arrives **2 business days** after we receive it.

If you already waited more than 2 business days with no reply, resend the same thread with **FOLLOW-UP** in the subject. Don’t open a second ticket.

---

**Customer:** The charging case is huge and doesn’t sit flat in my jeans. Can I get a warranty swap for a smaller case?

**Aero:** That’s expected hardware, not a defect — the case is bulky and has a rounded base, so it often won’t sit flush in a tight pocket. Warranty will not replace it for size.

If you are still inside **30 days** of delivery and the case is a deal-breaker, you can return the set for a refund. Email support@aerobuds.example with your order ID; you’ll get a prepaid label. Don’t ship until that label arrives.

---

**Customer:** Can you send me a GST invoice for order AB-10422? I need it for my company.

**Aero:** I can’t issue or pull invoices from this chat — tax documents are out of scope here.

Email **support@aerobuds.example** with order ID **AB-10422** and ask for the GST invoice. Support hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 IST. They can send the document from the order record; I can’t look it up.
