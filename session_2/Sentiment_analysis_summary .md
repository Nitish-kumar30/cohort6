### 3. Overall Customer Sentiment

Customer opinion is evenly split three ways, with no single sentiment dominating overall. Reviewers who are happy tend to praise sound quality, comfort, battery life, and value relative to price, often expressing strong repurchase intent. Reviewers who are negative most often cite fit issues, unreliable Bluetooth connectivity, app instability, and disappointing after-sales support. A notable share of reviews are mixed or ambivalent — customers frequently weigh a genuine strength (e.g., call quality, price-to-performance) against a genuine weakness (e.g., music fidelity, customer service, pricing changes) and land on a "take it or leave it" verdict rather than a clear recommendation. This suggests the product performs reasonably on core audio functionality but has inconsistent quality control (fit, firmware, connectivity) and a customer support experience that undermines otherwise positive product impressions.

### 4. Three Most Important Product Issues Mentioned

1. **Fit and physical comfort inconsistency** — Multiple reviewers reported ear tips falling out, poor seal at different tip sizes, and general discomfort during activity (Reviews 7, 10, 14), directly undermining usability during exercise or daily wear.
2. **Connectivity and reliability problems** — Buds disconnecting, dropped audio channels, and unreliable Bluetooth performance were recurring complaints, sometimes severe enough to prompt returns (Reviews 2, 5, 11).
